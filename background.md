## How the fish moves

* **Tail:** a motor-driven crank (or a servo) swings the tail left and right. Faster beats give more thrust.
* **Pectoral fins:** one servo tilts both side fins together to climb or dive.
* **Dorsal rudder:** a servo turns the top fin to steer.

## How it stays dry

Only the sealed pod must be watertight. It uses a plug-in end cap with two O-rings, and each shaft passes through two greased O-rings.
