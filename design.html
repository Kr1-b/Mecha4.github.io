    Does it need gears?

    No custom gears. The N20 motor and the servos already have gearboxes inside. The crank turns the motor's spin into the tail's back-and-forth swing, and a short pushrod links the fin servo to the fin shaft.

    Which kit parts won't work underwater

    The ultrasonic sensor, PIR, IR obstacle sensor and the displays are useful for bench testing only. Ultrasonic and IR don't work through water and the tube wall, and radio barely does either. So run the fish on a pre-programmed swim pattern, or use a thin tether to the kit's joystick.

    How it fits together

    The electronics mount on the sled, the sled slides into the dry pod, and the end cap plugs in behind it. The pod drops into the bottom skin half, the top half closes over it, and three screws from the belly hold it shut.

    The three moving shafts go in last, from outside: the rudder shaft from the top, the fin cross shaft through both sides and the fin lever, and the tail shaft through the end cap into the crank.

    In Inventor: open fish_assembly.step with File → Open to get every part in place. To simulate, add revolute joints on the rudder shaft (vertical), fin shaft (sideways), tail shaft (lengthwise) and tail hinge pin, plus a slot joint between the crank pin and the tail lever.
