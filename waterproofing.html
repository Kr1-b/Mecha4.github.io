Waterproofing

Water has three ways into the dry box. Each one gets its own seal.

  • 1The lidA 2 mm O-ring cord sits in a groove on the flange. Ten M3 screws clamp the flat lid down and squeeze it about 25%.
  • 2Fin and rudder shaftsEach 3 mm shaft runs through a boss with two internal O-ring grooves. The shafts only swing back and forth, so greased O-rings hold well at pool depth.
  • 3Tail crank shaftThis one spins nonstop, which wears O-rings. Use the rear gland to start; if it weeps, switch to a magnetic coupling so no shaft passes through the wall at all.

O-ring squeeze check

An O-ring seals because it gets squashed. Too little and it leaks, too much and it tears or jams the servo. Try the two seals in this design, or your own numbers.

Will it float or sink?

A sealed box full of air floats hard. You want the finished fish just barely positive, so it rises on its own if the battery dies. The defaults come from the CAD: the dry box and lid displace about 948 cm³, and printed solid in PETG they weigh about 400 g.

Printing for water

  • Print the dry box and lid in PETG or ASA, not PLA (PLA softens and absorbs water over time).
  • Use at least 5 walls and 100% infill around the flange and the glands. Normal FDM walls leak through the layer lines otherwise.
  • Print the box upright so the flange face is the last, smoothest layer. Lightly sand the face flat.
  • Coat the outside with a thin layer of epoxy (e.g. XTC-3D) to close the layer lines.
  • Ream the 3 mm shaft bores with a 3 mm drill bit after printing so the shafts turn freely.
  • A resin printer gives watertight walls and accurate O-ring grooves straight off the printer, if you can get one.

Leak test before electronics

  1. Assemble the box, O-rings, shafts and lid with no electronics inside. Put a dry tissue in the box.
  2. Tighten the lid screws in a cross pattern, a little at a time.
  3. Weigh it down and hold it at the bottom of a bucket for 30 minutes, turning the shafts by hand.
  4. Open it and check the tissue. Any wet spot shows you which seal leaked.
  5. Fix that seal and repeat until the tissue stays dry, then fit the electronics with a silica gel pack.
